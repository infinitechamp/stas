About STAS

Powering possibilities

STAS Intelligent Systems is a Kenyan technology and consulting company. Our crowned lion stands for leadership, reliability and courage: we aim to be the partner you can trust to build things properly and stand behind them.

We work with businesses, organisations, schools and individuals across Kenya, combining software development, cloud services and consulting with hands-on training and research support.

  • Reliable. Clear scope, honest timelines, work that lasts.
  • Practical. Solutions sized to your budget and your team.
  • Local. Built for Kenyan businesses, with M-Pesa and local workflows in mind.
  • Supportive. We stay available after launch.
