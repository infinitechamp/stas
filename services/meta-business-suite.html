Meta Business Suite & Ads

Plenty of businesses have posted on Facebook and Instagram for years without a proper setup behind them. We connect your Page, Instagram and WhatsApp Business in Meta Business Suite, set up ad accounts you own and control, and run campaigns that reach the people who can actually buy from you.

What's included

  • Meta Business Suite setup: Facebook Page, Instagram and WhatsApp Business linked in one place
  • Ad account created under your own business, using your own payment method
  • Campaigns built in Ads Manager instead of the quick "Boost" button
  • Local targeting by distance from your shop, age and interests
  • Click-to-WhatsApp, lead and website-traffic campaigns
  • Guidance on simple phone-shot videos and photos that work
  • Meta Pixel and conversion tracking where it is needed
  • Budget setup, ongoing monitoring and plain-language reports

You keep full ownership of your Page, accounts and ad account. Your ad budget is paid directly to Meta from your own card, and our management fee is agreed with you upfront. We do not promise specific results, but we track what works and keep improving it.

How we set you up

  1. 1

    Connect everything

    Your Facebook Page, Instagram and WhatsApp Business are linked in one Meta Business Suite, so posts, messages and ads live in one place.

  2. 2

    You own the accounts

    The ad account is created in your name with your own card. You stay the admin, so no shared passwords and no dependence on one person.

  3. 3

    Skip the Boost button

    Boosting is quick but gives you little control. We build campaigns in Ads Manager, where you decide who sees the ad and what it should achieve.

  4. 4

    Choose the audience

    We target people near your business, in the right age group and with relevant interests, not the whole country.

  5. 5

    Create the content

    Usually a short video on your own phone: you, your product, your shop. Real and personal beats polished.

  6. 6

    Set the budget

    We agree a daily or total spend that suits you, then watch the results, adjust, and report back in plain language.
